|   PA7   |  A15(GPIO2)  |           E1B            |

|  PB13   |  F13(GPIO6)  |           AIN2           |
|   PA0   |  E13(GPIO8)  |           PWMA           |
|   PB0   | P6(ADC_DOUT) |         SENEOR1          |
